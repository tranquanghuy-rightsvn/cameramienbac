Giới thiệu AXIS M5075

AXIS M5075 là camera PTZ cỡ nhỏ (đường kính khoảng 130 mm) của Axis, độ phân giải Full HD 1080p, zoom quang 5x và zoom số 12x, tự động lấy nét, có WDR. Camera tích hợp micro hỗ trợ phân tích âm thanh như phát hiện tiếng la hét, kính vỡ.

Nén Zipstream H.264/H.265 giảm dung lượng lưu trữ, khe thẻ nhớ, cấp nguồn PoE, tích hợp tính năng an ninh mạng — phù hợp cửa hàng, sảnh, phòng học cần xoay quét kín đáo.

Thông tin từ hãng sản xuất (tiếng Anh)

Small size. Small price

With a diameter of just 130 x 63 mm (5.1 x 2.5 in), this compact and discreet palm-sized PTZ camera blends discreetly into any environment. It comes at a price point that’s nothing if not reasonable. Featuring a built-in microphone, it enables audio analytics such as scream and glass break detection. Thanks to Zipstream with H.264/H.265 it significantly reduces storage and bandwidth without compromising image quality. This affordable camera can be used with installation material from the previous model (AXIS M5054). Furthermore, built-in cybersecurity features help prevent unauthorized access and safeguard your system.

See it all – and see it clearly

AXIS M5075 delivers sharp, detailed video inHDTV 1080p/2 MPresolution with great image usability. Thanks to PTZ functionality with 5x optical zoom and 12x digital zoom you can benefit from sweeping overviews and zoom in to get the details. Plus, autofocus ensures detailed, sharp images—every time. WithWide Dynamic Range (WDR) technology,it preserves important forensic detail when there’s both dark and light areas in the samescene. Furthermore, constant angle focusing ensures the field of view doesn’t change even when the focus changes.

Built-in analytics

This plug & play functionality is a cost-effective way to gain insight for more efficient and effective security. It includes built-in analytics that transforms the camera into a business tool to help you make smart decisions about operations and resource allocation. For instance, it includes audio volume detection which uses the built-in microphone to trigger an event. Scream detection triggers an event when the microphone detects a scream in the camera's proximity. Furthermore, AXIS Video motion detection triggers an event when movement is detected in a predefined area.
